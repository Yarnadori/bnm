# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Accepting feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.

To report a concern, contact the project maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be handled with reasonable care and confidentiality.
